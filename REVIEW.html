Implementation architecture

Actual executable modification

; Baseline: B8 00 00 17 5A C3
mov eax, 0x5A170000
ret

; Candidate: B8 0F 00 17 5A C3
mov eax, 0x5A17000F
ret

The low four bits enable modeled map selection, fuel compensation, launch torque limiting and flat-shift torque limiting. The feature gate is intentionally present in owned code; it is not an OEM hook discovered by reverse engineering.
